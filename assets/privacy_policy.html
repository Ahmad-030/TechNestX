Privacy Policy

TaskNestX — ZaraGamesHub

🔒

Your Privacy is Our Priority

TaskNestX is built with privacy-first principles. We believe your personal productivity data belongs to you — and only you.

✅ TaskNestX works 100% offline. No internet connection required. No accounts needed.
📦

Information We Collect

We collect absolutely nothing.

TaskNestX does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data you create — tasks, points, badges, and streaks — is stored exclusively on your own device using local storage (SharedPreferences).

  • No name, email, or account required
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No crash reporting sent externally
  • No advertisements or ad SDKs
📱

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device:

  • Tasks — titles, descriptions, priorities, tags, due dates
  • Gamification — points, badges, streaks, level progress
  • Preferences — theme selection (dark/light)

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data.

🚫

Permissions

TaskNestX requires zero permissions. We do not request access to:

  • Camera, microphone, or photos
  • Contacts or calendar
  • Location services
  • Network access
  • Storage (beyond app-private SharedPreferences)
👶

Children's Privacy

TaskNestX does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is ever collected or transmitted, the app is safe for all ages.

🔄

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted within the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
